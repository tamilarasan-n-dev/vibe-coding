01 / About

Engineering reliable systems
at every layer.

From database internals to distributed orchestration, I build the infrastructure that keeps products alive when everything else breaks.

I'm a backend software engineer focused on the unglamorous, mission-critical work: AI pipelines, distributed systems, and performance-critical infrastructure.

My days are spent reasoning about consistency models, tuning query planners, designing resilient message flows, and shipping code that survives production at scale. I care about the details most people don't see — p99 latency, graceful degradation, backpressure, and the quiet reliability of a well-designed system.

I work across Rust, TypeScript, Go, Python, and a deep stack of Postgres, Redis, Kafka, DuckDB, and AWS. When I'm not shipping, I'm experimenting with Rust, reading papers on distributed consensus, or profiling something that shouldn't be that slow.
