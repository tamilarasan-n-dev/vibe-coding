01 · ABOUT

I build the parts of a system nobody notices — until they break.

I'm a backend engineer working at the intersection of AI infrastructure and distributed systems. Most of my time goes into the unglamorous layer underneath a product: request routing, tenant isolation, connection pooling, queueing, and the data pipelines that feed everything above them.

At Highbrow Technology, I work on CleverHire.ai, an AI-powered interview platform — designing the voice-agent orchestration, the multi-tenant data layer, and the scoring pipelines that turn a conversation into a structured hiring decision.

"I enjoy building systems that keep working when everything else breaks."

Outside of product work, I spend time experimenting with Rust and DuckDB for large-scale data processing, reading up on database internals, and occasionally helping other engineers get their resumes past the same ATS filters I have to think about myself.
